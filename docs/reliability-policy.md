# Reliability Policy

The workbench prevents unsupported claims, scope creep, cascading errors, context loss, and unsafe tool use.

- Chat is not the source of truth; repository records are.
- Every material action requires evidence and a verification record.
- Agents may propose improvements but may not self-override approval rules.
- Failed or uncertain actions must be logged with a concrete recovery step.
- Cost, expected value, and decision basis must be explicit for commercial work.
